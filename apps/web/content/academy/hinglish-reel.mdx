## Why this track

A Hinglish reel is the single most common first export on the platform: a phone-shot
clip, captions that read naturally in mixed Hindi/English, and a 9:16 export ready to
post. This track takes you through it start to finish.

### 1. Upload your raw clip

Start a new project and upload any clip under a few minutes — a vlog segment, a talking
head, a reaction. Nothing about this track requires clean audio; the transcript step is
built for real phone audio.

### 2. Review the transcript

The transcript keeps Hinglish exactly as spoken rather than force-translating it, and
every word carries both a roman and a native-script rendering you can switch between.
Correct the odd misheard word by clicking it and retyping — the rest of the transcript
is untouched.

### 3. Pick a caption style

Open the style panel and try two or three of the bundled 9:16 presets. Captions update
live against your footage so you can see exactly what viewers will see before you commit.

### 4. Export your reel

Export at 1080p. This step is the one exception to "Mark done" in this track — finishing
a real export marks it automatically, because that is the actual outcome the track is
named for.

**Reward:** completing every step once grants 15 credits to your workspace, automatically.
