## Why this track

Editors who already live inside Premiere don't want a second timeline to maintain —
they want captions that follow the cut they're already making. The panel plugin
mirrors your project's transcript and styles directly inside Premiere.

### 1. Install the Premiere panel

Install the panel from the plugin settings in your account, then open Premiere and
dock the panel next to your normal timeline and Program monitor.

### 2. Link a cloud project

Sign in from the panel and select the cloud project matching the sequence you're
editing. The transcript and current caption styling load into the panel immediately.

### 3. Adjust captions from the panel

Everything you'd do in the web editor — nudging a caption's timing, swapping a style,
hiding a filler word — is available from the panel, applied to the same underlying
document the web app edits.

### 4. Sync changes back

When you trim or re-order clips in Premiere, sync from the panel to fold those cuts
back into the project so caption timing tracks your edit rather than the original
recording.
