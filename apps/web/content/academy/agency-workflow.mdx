## Why this track

Agencies run several clients through one account, and the parts that make that
workable are role-scoped access, per-client tagging and brand kits that don't have
to be re-applied by hand every time.

### 1. Invite your team

From Team settings, invite editors and assign each one a role — from view-only
review access up to full editing rights — so a freelancer only ever sees what
they're meant to.

### 2. Tag projects by client

Client tags group projects for reporting and, on team plans, for pooled credit
tracking — so you always know which client a given spend belongs to.

### 3. Apply a client's brand kit

Set up a brand kit per client (fonts, colours, logo, watermark) once, then apply it
to every project for that client. Switching between clients is a style pick, not a
manual re-brand.

### 4. Export a client report

Deliver the finished export. Because the project is tagged, the export shows up
correctly attributed the moment it completes — no manual reconciliation.

**Reward:** completing every step once grants 25 credits to your workspace, automatically.
