## Why this track

Long-form audio is where most creators lose the most time — scrubbing back and forth
looking for the moment worth clipping. This track is about working from the transcript
instead of the waveform.

### 1. Upload the full episode

Upload the whole recording, however long it runs. Diarisation and transcription both
scale to full-length episodes, not just short clips.

### 2. Confirm speaker labels

Each distinct voice gets its own speaker id and colour. Rename "Speaker 1" and
"Speaker 2" to the real names once, and every caption in the project picks it up —
no per-line renaming.

### 3. Mark chapter boundaries

Scan the transcript for the moments where the conversation turns — a new question,
a new guest, a new topic — and split there. Chapters are just named ranges on top of
the same transcript; nothing about the underlying edit changes.

### 4. Export your clips

Export each chapter independently. Because the source project already carries styles,
speaker names and any edits you made, every clip export inherits them without re-work.

**Reward:** completing every step once grants 20 credits to your workspace, automatically.
