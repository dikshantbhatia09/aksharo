## Premiere Pro

The Premiere panel mirrors your project's transcript and caption styling directly
inside a Premiere sequence, and can push your Premiere-side trims back to the cloud
project — see the "Captions inside Premiere" Academy track for a full walkthrough.

## After Effects

The After Effects panel is for style and template work: pull a project's captions in
as AE text layers driven by the same style presets, for effects work beyond what the
built-in style panel covers.

## DaVinci Resolve

The Resolve panel focuses on colour-graded projects — bring captions in as a Resolve
subtitle track that follows your existing timeline cuts.

## Licence keys

Each plugin activates against a licence key tied to your workspace, generated from
Settings → Plugins → Keys. A key can be deactivated from one machine and reactivated
on another at any time.
