## Where credits come from

Your plan grants a set number of credits every billing period. On top of that, you can
buy top-ups, redeem a pass, or earn credits by referring a friend or completing an
Academy track — every source shows up in your credit history with where it came from.

## How credits are spent

AI processing (transcription, translation, auto-cut passes) and rendering both spend
credits, metered by actual usage rather than a flat per-export price. You'll see an
estimate before a job starts.

## Running low

You'll get a low-credits notice with enough headroom to top up before anything is
blocked. A job that's already running is never interrupted for running out mid-way —
only new jobs are held until you have enough.

## Expiry

Monthly plan credits expire at the end of the period they were granted for; top-ups,
passes and referral/Academy credits do not expire.
