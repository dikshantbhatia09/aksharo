## Supported formats

Video (mp4, mov, mkv) and audio (wav, mp3, m4a) uploads are both accepted. There is no
hard length limit — transcription and diarisation both scale to full-length recordings.

## What happens after upload

Uploading kicks off media probing, a lower-resolution proxy for fast scrubbing, and a
16 kHz mono audio extraction used for transcription. You can start reviewing the
transcript as soon as it's ready — you don't have to wait for the proxy.

## If an upload fails

- Check your connection — uploads resume automatically after a brief drop, but not
  after the tab is closed.
- Very large files (multi-hour recordings) can take longer to probe; the project shows
  a clear "processing" state rather than looking stuck.
