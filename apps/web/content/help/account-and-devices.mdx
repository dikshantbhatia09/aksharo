## Signing in

Sign in with a magic link sent to your email, or a password if you've set one. Both
paths land you in the same account.

## New device approval

Signing in from a device we haven't seen before sends an approval notice so you can
confirm it was you. This is separate from, and in addition to, the sign-in link or
password itself.

## Managing sessions and devices

Settings → Devices lists every signed-in device and lets you sign one out remotely —
useful if you've lost a device or handed one back.

## Deleting your account

Account deletion is available from Settings → Privacy. It starts a data-subject
request that is processed within the timelines the law requires, and you'll get a
confirmation once it's complete.
