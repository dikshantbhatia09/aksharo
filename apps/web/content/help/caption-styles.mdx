## Bundled presets

The style panel ships with a set of presets tuned for 9:16, 1:1 and 16:9 exports.
Selecting one applies it to every caption in the project instantly — nothing is
baked in until you export.

## Overriding one segment

You can override font, colour, position or emphasis on a single caption segment
without changing the rest of the project's style — useful for emphasising one word
in one line without restyling the whole video.

## Saving your own style

Once you've tuned a preset to your liking, save it as your own named style. It then
appears alongside the bundled presets for every future project in the workspace.

## Brand kits

A brand kit bundles a style with your logo, watermark and fonts, and can be applied
per client or per project — see "Brand kits and client work" for the agency workflow.
