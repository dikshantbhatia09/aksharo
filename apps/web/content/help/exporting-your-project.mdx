## Choosing a resolution

Export at up to 1080p on most plans; higher resolutions are available on plans that
include them. Pick the aspect ratio (9:16, 1:1, 16:9) that matches where you're
publishing — the canvas and captions were already set up for it while editing.

## Watermarks

Free-plan exports carry a small watermark. Your very first export is watermark-free
regardless of plan, as a one-time introduction to what an unwatermarked export looks
like.

## What uses credits

Export rendering itself, along with the AI passes that produced auto-cuts, reframes or
sound effects, are metered in credits. The cost is estimated and held before the job
starts and settled to the actual amount once it finishes — you're never charged more
than the estimate without being asked.

## Export failed or looks wrong

If an export fails, it does not consume the credit hold — the amount is released
automatically. If the result looks wrong (missing captions, wrong aspect), check the
project's style and canvas settings before re-exporting; a support ticket can include
your last export attempts automatically if you need us to look at it.
