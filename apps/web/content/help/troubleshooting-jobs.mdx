## First, check the job's status

Every job (transcription, an AI pass, a render) shows live progress. A job that has
been "processing" far longer than usual for its length is worth a support ticket; one
that shows "failed" already released any credits it held.

## Common causes

- A very large upload can take longer to probe than a typical clip — this is normal,
  not stuck.
- A browser export depends on your machine's GPU; if it repeatedly fails, try a
  server-side export instead from the same export dialog.

## Contacting support

Settings → Support lets you open a ticket and, if you agree, attach a diagnostics
bundle — your app version, browser/OS, workspace id and your last 10 job ids with
their statuses. It never includes your media or transcript content, and you can send
the ticket without it if you'd rather not share it.
