## Editing a word

Click any word to edit its text in place. Editing the transcript is the primary way to
edit your video — cutting a word from the transcript cuts it from the export too.

## Filler words

Words the model marks as filler ("um", "like") are flagged but not removed
automatically — review them and delete the ones you don't want before exporting.

## Roman and native script

For mixed-language speech, every word can carry both a roman-script and a
native-script rendering. Use the script toggle to switch which one your captions
display without retyping anything.

## Undo

Every transcript edit is a normal, undoable operation — there is no separate
"transcript mode" to leave before undo works again.
