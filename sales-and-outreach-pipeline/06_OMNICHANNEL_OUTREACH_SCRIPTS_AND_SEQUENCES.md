## 6. Email Deliverability & Anti-Spam Guardrails

To ensure 99%+ inbox placement in 2026:
1. **Never send cold outreach from your primary root domain:**  
   If your company domain is `crestmondtechnologies.com`, buy secondary domains: `getaksharo.com`, `aksharostudio.com`, `tryaksharo.com`.
2. **Setup DNS Authentication:**  
   Configure SPF, DKIM, DMARC (`p=quarantine` or `p=none`), and custom tracking domain.
3. **Warmup Protocol:**  
   Warm all inboxes for 14 days using automated warmup tools (Instantly or Smartlead).
4. **Volume Limits:**  
   Never exceed **30 to 40 cold emails per day per email inbox**. Rotate across 3 inboxes to send 100 emails/day safely.
5. **Clean Plaintext Format:**  
   Send initial cold emails in clean, plain text with at most 1 link. Avoid heavy HTML templates, images, and sales buzzwords (*"guarantee", "buy now", "free money"*).
