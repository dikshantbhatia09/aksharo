## How to Pitch & Close Marketing & Podcast Agencies as Wholesale Partners

**Document Type:** 1-Page Executive Wholesale Proposal  
**Audience:** Owners & Heads of Production at Podcast Production & PR Agencies

## 1. The Executive Value Proposition (The Pitch Memo)

```text
MEMORANDUM: White-Label Video Production Partnership
TO: [Agency Owner Name], [Agency Name]
FROM: [Your Name], Founder, Aksharo Studio
SUBJECT: Scaling [Agency Name]'s Video Repurposing Margins with Zero Production Bottlenecks

1. THE SITUATION
Your agency produces world-class audio, strategy, and branding for top-tier podcast shows and corporate clients. However, offering high-volume short-form video (Shorts, Reels, TikTok, LinkedIn Video) in-house introduces severe operational friction:
- High editor payroll ($1,500 - $3,000/month per full-time editor).
- Slow turnaround times (5 to 7 days per batch).
- Constant revision churn and project management bottlenecks.

2. THE WHITE-LABEL SOLUTION
Aksharo Studio operates as your agency's dedicated, invisible video fulfillment engine.
- We ingest raw client audio/video.
- We extract the 5-7 highest-impact viral hooks.
- We format them into 9:16 vertical 4K videos with dynamic kinetic typography, custom brand palettes, and optimized pacing.
- We deliver ready-to-publish video packages + social copy in guaranteed 36 hours.
- 100% white-labeled under [Agency Name]'s brand. Your clients never know we exist.

3. THE WHOLESALE UNIT ECONOMICS
- You charge your client: $2,500 - $3,500 / month per show.
- Wholesale fee to Aksharo: $900 / month per show (flat).
- Net Profit to [Agency Name]: $1,600 - $2,600 / month per show.
- Your time spent editing: ZERO HOURS.

4. GUARANTEED 36-HOUR SLA & QUALITY PROOF
Because our pipeline is built on a proprietary GPU rendering stack with dedicated senior human editorial QC, we guarantee 36-hour delivery SLAs and 0% missed deadlines.

5. NEXT STEP (THE FREE PILOT EPISODE)
Send us the raw footage of 1 upcoming client episode. We will deliver a complete 6-clip video repurposing package 100% free under your agency's branding so you can inspect our quality firsthand.
```

## 2. Discovery Call Script for Agency Owners (15 Minutes)

### Step 1: Establish Common Ground
> *"Hey [Name], thanks for connecting. The reason I reached out is that we work exclusively with high-end agencies like yours. You guys do an incredible job on the audio and client strategy side, but from speaking with a lot of agency heads, short-form video editing is usually the biggest operational headache in the company. How are you guys currently handling the short-form video clips for your shows?"*

### Step 2: Uncover the Bottleneck
> *"How much time is your senior team spending reviewing drafts, fixing bad subtitle typos, or chasing down freelance editors?"*  
> *(They will usually say: 'It's a huge pain. We spend way too much time managing editors.')*

### Step 3: Present the Wholesale Retainer
> *"That's exactly why we built our wholesale engine. We don't deal with the end clients. We integrate directly into your agency Slack or Google Drive.*  
> *When your team finishes recording an episode, you drop the link in our shared channel.*  
> *Within 36 hours, you get back 6 high-retention vertical clips and LinkedIn/X copy formatted to your client's exact brand guidelines.*  
> *We charge a flat wholesale fee of \$900 per client show per month. You can charge your client \$2,500 to \$3,500, pocket the \$1,600+ margin, and never manage another video editor again."*

### Step 4: The Immediate Test
> *"Let's test it on one show. Pick your most demanding client or your upcoming episode. Drop us the file. We will produce the first 6 clips completely on us. If you love the speed and quality, we can onboard your other client shows next week. Sound fair?"*
