## 2. Cold Email Body Variations

>  
> Hi {{First_Name}},  
>  
> Big fan of {{Show_Name}}—especially your recent episode with {{Guest_Name}} exploring {{Topic}}.  
>  
> I noticed you haven't repurposed the 45-second highlight at {{Timestamp}} into a vertical video yet, so rather than pitching you services, my team went ahead and formatted it into a 9:16 clip with kinetic captions for you:  
>  
> **[Watch 45-Second Preview & Download 4K Clip: {{Link}}]**  
>  
> It's 100% free, unwatermarked, and ready to post to your LinkedIn or Shorts today.  
>  
> If you like the style and want someone to handle this for all your episodes every week on autopilot, let me know. Either way, hope the clip crushes it!  
>  
> Best,  
> **{{Your_Name}}**

## 3. High-Converting Follow-Up Angles

### Follow-Up 1: The "Native Distribution Hook" (Sent 3 days later)
> *"Quick thought {{First_Name}}—if you end up posting that clip, try opening your LinkedIn post with this line:  
> '{{Suggested_Hook}}'  
> That usually drives 2–3x higher click-through on video feeds. Did you get a chance to check out the clip?"*

### Follow-Up 2: The "Case Study & Speed Proof" (Sent 7 days later)
> *"Hey {{First_Name}}, quick stat: our partners usually see a 40% jump in organic impressions in their first 30 days simply because we post daily short-form clips consistently without eating any of their time.  
> Would you be open to a 10-minute chat this Thursday to see what an automated engine would look like for {{Company_Name}}?"*

> *"Hey {{First_Name}}, assuming short-form video isn't on your radar for {{Company_Name}} right now—completely understood!  
> I'll stop following up. The free clip remains yours forever. If you ever want to turn your long-form talks into an omnipresent content engine down the road, feel free to reach back out anytime. Cheers!"*
