## Long description

Aksharo Panel brings Hinglish (Hindi-English code-switched) transcription, styled captions,
and one-click cut/zoom/audio-cleanup application straight into Premiere Pro's Text-Based
Editing and timeline — no export/import round-trip. Sign in once (device-code pairing, or an
offline licence key on a locked-down machine) and every project on your plan is available
from the panel.

- Transcript lands in Premiere's own Text-Based Editing panel.
- MOGRT captions, alpha overlays, or SRT-to-bin — pick the workflow that matches your delivery.
- Accepted cuts, zooms and cleaned audio apply as real timeline edits in one transaction, with
  progress and a rollback on any failure.
- One credit pool across the web app and every plugin — no per-plugin subscription.
